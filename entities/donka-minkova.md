# Donka Minkova

Author of *Alliteration and Sound Change in Early English* (CUP, 2003) and *A Historical Phonology of English* (2014). Her chapter on velar palatalization argues from alliteration that [ɣ] split into /j/ and /ɡ/ around the middle of the tenth century. Known to this vault only through [[references/kijak-2022-velar-palatalization]] and a catalogue entry; her book was not read.
