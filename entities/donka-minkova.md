# Donka Minkova

Author of *Alliteration and Sound Change in Early English* (CUP, 2003) and *A Historical Phonology of English* (2014). Her chapter on velar palatalization argues from alliteration that [ɣ] split into /j/ and /ɡ/ around the middle of the tenth century. Known to this vault only through [[references/kijak-2022-velar-palatalization]] and a catalogue entry; her book was not read.

With Robert Stockwell she wrote “The English Vowel Shift: Problems of Coherence and Explanation,” in Dieter Kastovsky and Gero Bauer (eds.), *Luick Revisited* (Tübingen: Narr, 1988), 355–395. That article was not read. The title and pages are from her UCLA bibliography. [[sources/prichard-2014-northern-gvs|Prichard]] quotes their conclusion that the Great Vowel Shift is “the linguist’s creation through hindsight.” See [[concepts/great-vowel-shift]].
