# Richard M. Hogg

Author of *A Grammar of Old English*, volume 1, *Phonology* (1992), and, with R. D. Fulk, volume 2, *Morphology* (2011). The palatalization sections of volume 1 are on [[references/hogg-1992-palatalization]]. "Old English palatalization," *Transactions of the Philological Society* 77 (1979): 89–113, is still unread; the grammar cites pp. 93–96 and 103–4. The 1979 article is also the one later writers use when they deny that Old English and Old Frisian palatalization must be a single shared change.
