# Ari Þorgilsson

Krause & Slocum (EIEOL lesson 1): 1067–1148. Snorri’s preface to *Heimskringla* is the main later sketch of his life. He dates, quotes witnesses, and interrupts a sentence to name them — unlike much saga prose.

## Key Ideas

- A longer *Íslendingabók* with kings’ lives and genealogies is **lost**. The surviving *Libellus Islandorum* is also called *Íslendingabók*: settlement history, composed first for bishops Þorlákr and Ketill, shown to the priest Sæmundr, then rewritten without genealogy and kings’ lives, with later corrections. *Landnámabók* may draw on Ari rather than being co-authored by him. ^[ambiguous]
- Named authorities in the settlement chapter: foster-brother Teitr Ísleifsson; uncle Þorkell Gellisson; Þóríðr Snorradóttir.
- Style: plain, source-first. See [[references/islendingabok]].
