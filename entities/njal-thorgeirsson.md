# Njáll Þorgeirsson

Title figure of *Brennu-Njáls saga* ("Burnt Njáll's saga"). Njáll is wise and generous, but his impetuous sons kill Þráinn; he tries to end the feud by adopting Þráinn's son Hǫskuldr, whom the sons then kill. Hǫskuldr's kinsman Flosi takes revenge and burns Njáll's house with him inside; Kári later avenges them and is reconciled with Flosi. In the lesson excerpt ([[references/njals-saga-gunnars-last-stand]]) Njáll is only background; the centre is his friend Gunnarr. Course context: [[skills/ut-eieol-old-norse]].
