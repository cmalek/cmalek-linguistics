# Grettir the Strong

Outlaw of Iceland, traditionally 996-1031; the saga in its present form is 14th c., so how much is historical is unclear. His fights with the ghost Glámr and a troll-wife echo [[references/beowulf]]'s Grendel episodes. See [[references/grettis-saga-glam-fight]].
