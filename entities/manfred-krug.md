# Manfred Krug

Author of “(Great) vowel shifts present and past” (*Penn Working Papers in Linguistics*, 2003). He argues that the upper half of the Great Vowel Shift was a drag chain, motivated by glide insertion in frequent *I am* and *thou art* sequences, and he notes that direct evidence for that liaison is lacking. The paper’s address is Freiburg.

[[sources/schlueter-2012-emode-phonology|Schlüter]] sends the handbook treatment of the shift to his chapter in Bergs and Brinton. A 2012 chapter (pp. 756–776 of *English Historical Linguistics*) and a 2017 chapter in *Early Modern English* (DOI [10.1515/9783110525069-014](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110525069-014)) were not read. The 2017 abstract says consensus on the shift is limited.

See [[concepts/great-vowel-shift]] and [[sources/krug-2003-gvs-inception]].
