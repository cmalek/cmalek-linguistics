# The Year’s Work in English Studies

The journal publishes narrative bibliographic reviews of English language and literature scholarship. Its [[concepts/old-english|Old English]] coverage provides a useful independent set of headings for this map. [Journal](https://academic.oup.com/ywes).

The 2024 abstract was inspected; selected 2025/2026 pages were checked. Annual-review publication dates and the dates of works discussed must be distinguished. No full decade of review bibliographies was extracted.
