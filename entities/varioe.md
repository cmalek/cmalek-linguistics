# VARIOE

VARIOE is the University of Łódź lemmatisation of the [[entities/ycoe|YCOE]], led by Anna Cichosz with Piotr Pęzik, Maciej Grabski, Michał Adamczyk, Paulina Rybińska, and Aneta Ostrowska (2021). The morphological dictionary is at [varioe.pelcra.pl/morph](http://varioe.pelcra.pl/morph). A sister tool lists collocations.

The frequency-dictionary preface (Cichosz et al.) says the lemmas were assigned manually, five annotators working through forms grouped by part of speech, with the Dictionary of Old English A–I and Bosworth–Toller as the headword authorities. Ann Taylor allowed use of the YCOE tags, and the lemmas were sent back toward a lemmatised YCOE release. Spelling and morphological variants of one meaning were sometimes one lemma, because the project was built for phraseology. The preface points to the morphological dictionary for the full form list and for the details of that merging.

[[references/computational-study-of-old-english-2025|Martín Arista (2025)]] describes the result as about 30,000 lemmas and accuracy over 95 percent, with a hierarchy of YCOE tags, context, frequency, and manual review. Treat the percentage as his report.

VARIOE is an attested form inventory of prose. It does not generate unattested dialect cells, and the preface’s merging policy is the opposite of keeping every spelling as its own variant.
