# Snorri Sturluson

EIEOL lesson 2: 1179–1241. Two visits to Hákon Hákonarson; aligned with Hákon and Skúli, then out of favour after Skúli’s revolt (Skúli killed 1240). Snorri is killed in his own house on orders of Gizurr Þorvaldsson.

## Key Ideas

- Fairly certain: poems quoted in *Sturlunga saga* and *Hákonar saga Hákonarsonar*; *Heimskringla* likely compiled by him; **Prose Edda**. *Egils saga* is sometimes attached to him with **no direct evidence**. ^[ambiguous]
- *Prose Edda* parts: Prologue + *Gylfaginning* (myth); *Skáldskaparmál* (diction); *Háttatal* (metres). See [[references/prose-edda]].
- He writes as a **Christian** Icelander explaining older stories, not as a temple polytheist.
