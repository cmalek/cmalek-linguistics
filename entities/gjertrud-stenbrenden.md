# Gjertrud F. Stenbrenden

Author of *Long-Vowel Shifts in English, c. 1050–1700: Evidence from Spelling* (Cambridge University Press, 2016). The introduction argues from occasional spellings and treats the traditional Great Vowel Shift as one bundle inside a longer set of long-vowel changes. [[sources/ikegashira-2020-stenbrenden-review|Ikegashira’s review]] reports the book’s conclusion that the bundle is not a single process.

The introduction excerpt was read. The spelling chapters were not. No affiliation is recorded here. See [[concepts/great-vowel-shift]] and [[sources/stenbrenden-2016-long-vowel-shifts]].
