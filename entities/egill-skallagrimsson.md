# Egill Skallagrímsson

Hero of *Egils saga*, a historical figure with a knack for adventure and "raucous behaviour" per the UT course. A steward of Eiríkr Blóðøx tried to poison him for drinking the ale store; Egill killed the steward, later sued a friend of Eiríkr, was driven off, and in revenge killed that man and one of Eiríkr's sons. This began a long feud with Eiríkr and his queen Gunnhildr.

At York he is saved by his friend Arinbjǫrn and by a praise poem composed overnight ([[references/egils-saga-eirik-court-passage]]). He illustrates the saga motif of the warrior who is also a poet. ^[inferred] Poetic conventions: [[references/prose-edda]].
