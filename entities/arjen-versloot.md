# Arjen Pieter Versloot

Arjen Pieter Versloot is the author of [[sources/versloot-2023-yorkshire]] and [[sources/versloot-2026-reconstructing-old-english]]. The former addresses inherited northern forms; the latter proposes a revised early vowel chronology.

Their relevance to [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]] is explained in [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]]. The revised chronology is a proposal, not presented here as settled consensus.
