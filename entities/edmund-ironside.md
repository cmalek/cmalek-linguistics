# Edmund Ironside

Rebelled against [[entities/aethelred-the-unready]] in 1015, then held out against [[entities/cnut-the-great]] at Penselwood, Sherston, Brentford, and possibly Otford. Eadric Streona's flight at Assandun is blamed for the defeat. Partition (Wessex to Edmund) is reported by search summaries of Wikipedia; not in [[references/worldhistory-danish-conquest-of-england]]. See [[concepts/danish-conquest-of-england]].
