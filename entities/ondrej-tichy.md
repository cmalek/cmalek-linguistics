# Ondřej Tichý

Ondřej Tichý (Prague, Charles University) wrote the 2014 thesis *Morphological Analyser of Old English* and the 2017 article that summarizes it. See [[references/tichy-2017-morphological-analyser]].

He also produced the digitization of [[entities/bosworth-toller|Bosworth–Toller]] that the generator uses. The article says the analyzer was hosted, not publicly, beside the online dictionary on the Faculty of Arts server. The public dictionary is [bosworthtoller.com](https://bosworthtoller.com/). The 2026 lemmatiser card cites the electronic dictionary as Crist and Tichý 2014. Campbell’s 1972 addenda are absent from that online edition because they are not public domain.

The Perl implementation, released by Tichý under CC BY 4.0 and modified by Madeleine Thompson, is the upstream of [[projects/wyrdcraeft/concepts/morphology-generation|wyrdcraeft’s]] Python generator.
