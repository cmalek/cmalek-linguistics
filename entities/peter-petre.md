# Peter Petré

Peter Petré (University of Leuven; Research Foundation Flanders on the work cited here) writes on Old and Middle English copulas with Hubert Cuyckens.

- With Cuyckens, “Constructional change in Old and Middle English copular constructions and its impact on the lexicon,” *Folia Linguistica Historica* 30 (2009): 311–366. [[references/petre-cuyckens-2009-copulas]]
- “The functions of *weorðan* and its loss in the past tense in Old and Middle English,” *English Language and Linguistics* 14.3 (2010): 457–484. [[references/petre-2010-weorthan]]
- *Constructions and Environments: Copular, Passive, and Related Constructions in Old and Middle English* (Oxford University Press, 2014). OpenAlex lists the book and its chapters. This pass used the two articles, which Jones and Macleod also cite, and did not read the book.

Jones and Macleod (2017) take his chapter 2 as the literature review of the passive, and they dispute one grammaticalization test he uses: coordination of participles with adjectives.
