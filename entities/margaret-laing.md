# Margaret Laing

Margaret Laing (University of Edinburgh) is an editor of the *Linguistic Atlas of Early Middle English* (LAEME). For future time, the relevant chapter is “The reflexes of OE *beon* as a marker of futurity in early Middle English” (2010), summarized in [[references/laing-2010-beon-futurity]].

The abstract says Old English *b*-forms were already likelier than *s*-forms in future contexts, and that some early Middle English writing systems narrowed the singular *b*-present to future senses, with a northern extension into the plural where *ar-/*er-* forms existed. The chapter sits next to Ilse Wischer’s *bēon/wesan* study in the same Benjamins volume.
