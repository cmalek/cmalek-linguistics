# Stephen Laker

Stephen Laker is the author of [[sources/laker-2007-palatalization]] and [[sources/laker-2021-place-names]]. These works are relevant to [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]], especially the separation of palatalization and assibilation.

The 2007 relevant sections were read directly; the 2021 article was checked through its abstract and [[sources/versloot-2023-yorkshire]]. See [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]] for the evidence limits.
