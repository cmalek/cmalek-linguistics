# Dictionary of Old English

The Dictionary of Old English (DOE) at the University of Toronto writes entries from the [[entities/dictionary-of-old-english-corpus|Dictionary of Old English Corpus]], not by inheriting [[entities/bosworth-toller|Bosworth–Toller]]’s headword list. The About page says the corpus determines the headwords, the definitions, and the quotations, and that each entry gives the attested spellings, grammatical information, and sense divisions with citations. It is the Old English leg of a set the same page names with the Middle English Dictionary and the Oxford English Dictionary. The public site is [doe.artsci.utoronto.ca](https://doe.artsci.utoronto.ca/).

## How far the alphabet has got

The project’s own pages disagree by date, and the later page wins.

- The About page still says “as of 2018”: DOE: A to I online, and that nearly half the dictionary had been published while more than 60 percent of the entries had been written.
- [DOE: A to Le online](https://doe.artsci.utoronto.ca/?p=87) includes 750 entries for *la*, *læ*, and *le*, plus revised versions of the ten fascicles published from 1986 to 2018. The publications list dates this online edition 2024. The institutional door named in the La–Le announcement is https://dictionary.doe.utoronto.ca. The contact page prints https://dictionary.doe.utoronto.ca/doe/.
- The 2025 progress report says drafting of Li–Ly continued, about 575 entries, with publication hoped for in 2027 if drafting finishes in late 2026. Ma–Me was just begun: about 750 entries still undrafted.

Nerthus v5 links DOE headwords for A–I. That is Nerthus’s link range. It is not the 2024 online edition. Martín Arista’s 2025 survey said A–Le, which matches the project’s A to Le page.

The product page lists the same subscription shape as the corpus: institutions $200 a year, individuals $75 a year or 20 free logins. Institutional access is by campus IP. Individual access is email and password. Entries can be searched across fields, and the page says the dictionary links to the OED, the MED, and CoNE.

## Who edits it

The About page’s editorial succession is Angus Cameron, Ashley Crandell Amos, Antonette diPaolo Healey, Roy Liuzza, Haruko Momma, Robert Getz, and Stephen Pelle. Healey retired in 2014. The 2025 progress report names Getz and Pelle as editors, Cameron Laird as copy editor, Xi Wang, and Deanna Brooks as postdoctoral fellow. Healey is on the International Advisory Committee. The letterhead address is Robarts Library, room 14285, and the phone printed there is 416-978-8883.

## What a generator copies from an entry

Where an entry exists, the attested spellings and the grammatical label are the analyzed view of the corpus. [[references/computational-study-of-old-english-2025|Martín Arista (2025)]] describes a FORM line of spellings by case and number, a frequency, gender, and strong or weak where that applies. Letters past Le are not in the online edition. Those headwords still depend on Bosworth–Toller, Clark Hall, and Sweet, with the [[skills/using-doec|Web Corpus]] as the attestation check.
