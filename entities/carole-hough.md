# Carole Hough

Carole Hough, University of Glasgow, works on the boundary between English place-names and the [[concepts/old-english|Old English]] lexicon. The lecture read for this vault is [[sources/hough-2010-toponymicon-lexicon]]. It catalogues the kinds of words that survive only in names — plants, animals, landscape, farming — and then argues that a name-only element can belong to a toponymic register rather than to everyday speech. *Cumb* is her test case.

She is a co-editor of *The Vocabulary of English Place-Names*, the unfinished alphabetical replacement for Smith’s *English Place-Name Elements*. A 1995 paper of hers describes the database field from which a handlist of Old English words unattested outside place-names was meant to be generated. That separate handlist was not found as a completed book in this research.
