# Laker 2021: Palatalization in English and Scottish place-names

Stephen Laker, “Palatalization and assibilation of /k/ in English and Scottish place-names”, *NOWELE* 74(1) (2021), 80–115.

**Access:** full text not obtained. The abstract and bibliographic records were checked; the more detailed argument is known here through [[sources/versloot-2023-yorkshire]], pp. 135–136, which cites Laker pp. 107–110. Do not treat this page as an independent full-text verification of that argument.

The abstract identifies the scarcity of initial Ch- place-names in Scotland, northern England and much of the East Midlands and investigates differences between place-name and dialect evidence. This makes it directly relevant to claims of uniform regional assibilation.

Informs [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]] and [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]]. Author: [[entities/stephen-laker]].
