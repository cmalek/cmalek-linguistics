# Versloot 2023: The West Germanic Heritage of Yorkshire English

Arjen Versloot, “The West Germanic Heritage of Yorkshire English”, in *Medieval English in a Multilingual Context* (2023), DOI [10.1007/978-3-031-30947-2_5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-30947-2_5). Relevant discussion read at pp. 134–137.

Versloot distinguishes palatalization from assibilation and questions using a northern velar by itself to diagnose a Norse loan. Following Laker 2021, he discusses inherited northern /k/ and later diffusion of affricated forms from the south as alternatives to wholesale Scandinavian replacement.

**Limits:** the author explicitly leaves exact lexical conditioning for further research. This is a regional historical argument, not a per-word pronunciation dictionary. Its account is marked as a proposal wherever used in [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]].
