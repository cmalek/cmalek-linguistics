# Kirby and Harrington 2026: Acoustic-perceptual sound change

James Kirby and Jonathan Harrington, “Acoustic-Perceptual Factors in the Actuation of Sound Change”, *The Wiley Blackwell Companion to Diachronic and Historical Linguistics* (2026), DOI [10.1002/9781119898023.wbcdl063](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119898023.wbcdl063). Section 2.1 checked in the author-hosted PDF.

It discusses phonetic and perceptual mechanisms for velar-to-affricate change, using Old English among its illustrations.

**Limits:** a general sound-change chapter, not research establishing an OE lexical exception list. Its schematic OE examples must not be read as a carefully dated transcription of every OE stage.

Provides background for [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]] and [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]].
