# Þorgeirsson 2018: Egils saga and Heimskringla stylometry

Haukur Þorgeirsson, *European Journal of Scandinavian Studies* 48(1), 2018, pp. 1–18. The author-hosted paper was read.

## Findings

After a modern-Icelandic control test, the study finds *Egils saga* closest to both portions of *Heimskringla*, including comparisons against other kings’ sagas. The conclusion supports common authorship. Spelling and variant forms are normalized for comparison. The introduction records dissenting attribution arguments; pp. 16–17 discuss chronological stylistic differences and the conclusion.

## Limits

This is statistical attribution evidence, not a signed medieval attribution. Its normalization is a research choice, not an unmediated manuscript witness. The method does not date an individual court speech or authenticate a quoted poem.
