# Kranenburg 2019: The dative in the Chronicle

Jelle Kranenburg, “Dating the Outdated Dative” (thesis, Leiden University, 2019). Handle [1887/75405](http://hdl.handle.net/1887/75405). The repository text was read. The thesis is about Chronicle case marking. The sentences used here are the ones that name an ending.

He describes leveling of unstressed vowels toward a schwa, and he states a path for the dative plural from *-um* to *-an* and then to *-e*. The witness he gives for the *-um*/*-an* stage is Wulfstan’s *Sermo Lupi ad Anglos*, about 1014, which he says alternates the two endings. ^[extracted]

He quotes a 1001 Chronicle line, *mid þan scipan*, as *-an* where a dative plural is expected, and a Peterborough annal of 1070 with *scipe*. After 1121, in the First Continuation, he treats *-e* and the spread of prepositions as the Middle English stage. ^[extracted]

Informs [[concepts/old-english-early-late-writing]] and [[synthesis/Research: Early and Late Old English Spelling and Inflection]]. He does not print a table of Wulfstan’s *-um* and *-an* tokens in the passage that was used. The alternation is his statement, not a count reproduced here.
