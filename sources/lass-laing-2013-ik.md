# Lass and Laing 2013: The reflexes of Germanic ik

Roger Lass and Margaret Laing, “The early Middle English reflexes of Germanic *ik ‘I’: Unpacking the changes”, *Folia Linguistica Historica* 34 (2013), DOI [10.1515/flih.2013.004](https://doi.org/10.1515/flih.2013.004). Relevant sections 2.1–2.2 read.

The paper reconstructs multiple pathways for the pronoun, including affrication and a separate final-consonant weakening trajectory. Its kin/chin comparison illustrates why the history of a front vowel matters. The manuscript and geographical variation defeats a single uniform account of iċ.

**Limits:** this is a detailed study of one grammatical item, not an inventory of every c-word. It cautions against treating one classroom pronunciation as a universal historical value.
