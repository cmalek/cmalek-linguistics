# Versloot 2025–2026: Reconstructing the historical phonology of [[concepts/old-english|Old English]]

Arjen Pieter Versloot, “Reconstructing the historical phonology of Old English”, *English Language & Linguistics* 30(1), March 2026, 103–141; first published online 31 July 2025. Introduction, scope and conclusions checked in the open article.

The article proposes a revised vowel chronology using the early glossaries and [[concepts/old-english-dialects|Northumbrian]] evidence. Sections 1.1–1.2 identify unsettled palatalization questions and limit the immediate investigation chiefly to the history of stressed Proto-Germanic *a.

**Limits:** it is a recent proposal, not a consensus replacement for every standard sound law. It supplies no comprehensive c-exception list. Its print year must not be confused with its online publication date.

Informs the currency and uncertainty discussion in [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]] and [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]]. Author: [[entities/arjen-versloot]].
