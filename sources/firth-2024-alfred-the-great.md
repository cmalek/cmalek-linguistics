# Firth 2024: the medieval origins of Alfred’s “the Great”

Matthew Firth, *The English Historical Review* 139, no. 596 (2024), pp. 1–32.

## Findings

Firth identifies at least twenty-five medieval uses of Alfred’s byname in six English and two Icelandic texts. Roger of Wendover and Matthew Paris belong to the English evidence; *Egils saga* and *Landnámabók* contain *inn ríki*. He reads Elizabethan adoption as renewal of an older reputation, rather than invention. The article also places Parker’s 1574 Asser edition within Reformation interest in the past.

## Access and limits

The abstract and indexed article passages were consulted; direct publisher and repository PDF retrieval failed. The byname’s circulation does not establish how many medieval readers accessed Alfredian Old English prose. Routes between English and Icelandic traditions remain interpretive.
