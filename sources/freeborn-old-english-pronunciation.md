# Freeborn: Old English pronunciation commentary

Dennis Freeborn, supplementary “Text Commentary Book – OE pronunciation” for *From Old English to Standard English*, third edition, pp. 13–14. The publisher-hosted PDF sections and example tables were read.

The tables distinguish velar and palatal c and explicitly flag words containing both, including kitchen and crutch. They also compare OE forms with Modern English and northern outcomes.

**Limits:** this is teaching material. Its suggested Scandinavian explanation of some modern velars should be checked against the later regional work, and its unaccented spellings need care when normalized. The publication date was not independently verified here; a recent search-engine crawl is not a new edition.

Used for the lexical cross-check in [[concepts/old-english-c-palatalization]] and [[synthesis/Research: Old English C-Palatalization]].
