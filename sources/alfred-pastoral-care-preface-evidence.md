# Alfred’s Pastoral Care preface: programme and authorship

## Evidence read

Bucknell’s modern translation of the primary preface, Amy Faulkner’s Oxford introduction, and R. D. Fulk’s edition description were read on 28 October 2026.

## Findings

The preface proposes English literacy for eligible freeborn young people with means, then Latin for further training. It names clerical teachers and directs copies to bishoprics. Its account of Hebrew, Greek, and Latin translation supplies Christian precedent.

Faulkner dates distribution to 890–896 and treats the royal voice as persuasive rhetoric. She notes scholarly doubt about Alfred’s personal translation work. Bishops appear both as educational partners and potential recipients needing vernacular help.

## Limits

This is a stated programme, not an enrolment record. Royal attribution, implementation, and the authorship of other Alfredian translations remain separate questions. The Fulk volume description was consulted, not its full edition.
