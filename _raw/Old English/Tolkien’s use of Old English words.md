# Tolkien’s use of Old English words

### Rohan

* **Orthanc** \-\> orþanc \[skill\]  
* **Isengard** \-\> īsen \[steel, iron\] \+ geard \[guard, ward, watch\].   Gímli even says “and this is the iron stronghold we’re making for?”  
* **Eorl** \-\> eorl \[earl, nobleman, hero\]  
* **Éomund** \[Éomer’s father\] \-\> eo [from **eoh**, _horse_] \+ mund \[A protector, guardian\]  
* **Éomer** \-\>  eo \[from **eoh**, *horse*\] \+ mǣre \[great, excellent, distinguished, illustrious, sublime, splendid, celebrated, famous, widely known\] … maybe; seems a bit stretched.  Of course, he did, in fact, become great through his courage and because he became King of Rohan after Théoden died at Pellenor.
* **Éowyn** \[Éomund’s daughter\] \-\> eo [from **eoh**, _horse_] \+ wynn \[the best of a class, the pride of its kind\]  
* **éored**: ēored \[cavalry, a band, legion, troop\]  
* **Théoden** \-\> þēoden \[prince, king\]  
* **Thengel** \[Théoden’s father\] \-\> þengel \[prince, lord, leader\]  
* **Fengel** \[Thengel’s father\] \-\> fengel \[prince\]  
* “**Westu Théoden hál**” \-\> This seems like OE, but “westu” is not attested.  The closest we have is “west” which means West, or wēstan, waste, or possibly wīsa (captain, leader) and hāl makes more sense, meaning “whole, hale, well, in good health, sound, safe”; so maybe “Health \[to you\] Leader Théoden”  
* **Gríma** \-\> grīma \[a mask, visor, helmet\], which makes sense for a spy of Saruman  
* **Galmod** \[Gríma’s father\] \-\> gāl-mōd \[licentious\]  
* **Háma** \-\> hāma \[cricket, cicada\].   Maybe because he announces visitors?  
* **Edoras** \-\> edor \[A hedge, fence, place inclosed by a hedge, fold, dwelling, house\]; edoras \[masc. pl. acc.\]  
* **The Deeping Coomb** \-\> comb \[A low place inclosed with hills, a valley\]; dēop \[noun: feminine meaning deep\] \+ \-ing \[a suffix of feminine nouns denoting action\]  
* ***mearas*** \[the lords of horses, of which Shadowfax is the leader\] \-\> mearh \[horse\]; mearas \[masc. pl. acc.\]  
* **Dunland** \-\> dūn (mountainous, hilly; brown\] \+ \-lend \[land\]  
* **Hornburg** \-\> horn \[horn\] \+ burg \[fortress\]  
* **Helm the Hammerhand** \-\> helm \[a crown, the top, overshadowing foliage of trees – ironic when one thinks of the Huorns in the Coomb\]; contrast with *helmet* which is helm \+ et, where \-et is a French diminutive suffix  
* **The Mark** \-\> not OE, but Danish, where mark means “country”, thus Denmark, “Country of the Danes”  

### Other

* **Déagol** \-\> dīgol? \[secret\].  Déagol’s body was buried secretly by Sméagol and was never found.  
* **Sméagol** \~ āsmēagan \[imagine, understand\]; because he understood soon what the Ring could do for him?  Imagined its value?  
* **flet** \[the tree platforms of the Galadhrim\] \-\> flet \[floor, dwelling, hall\]  
* Beorn (from the Hobbit) \-\> beorn \[warrior\]  
* **Ent** \-\> ent (giant)  
* **Eärendil** \-\> ǣrendraca (messenger)/ǣrend (message) (?)  
* **mathom** \-\> māþum (treasure)
* **Galdor** -> galdor (incantation, enchantment).  Galdor was an elf of the Grey Havens that attended the Council of Elrond on behalf of Círdan.
* **athelas** -> æþel (adj: noble, eminent, excellent)
