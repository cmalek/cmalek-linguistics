summary: In August 991 Byrhtnoth, ealdorman of Essex, liege of Æthelræd I, encountered an army of vikings camped on Northey Island in the estuary of the River Blackwater near the town of Maldon, Essex. This island was (as it still is) connected to the mainland by a causeway which was covered at high tide. As the causeway was flooded when the armies met, battle could not be joined.  This is the story of the ensuing battle
