# UT Old Norse Online (EIEOL)

Todd B. Krause and Jonathan Slocum, UT LRC *Old Norse Online*. Audio: Sandra B. Straubhaar. Pair with [[skills/ut-eieol-old-english]].

## Key Ideas

**Culture:** *víkingr* is a Norse word (OE *wicing*) for seaborne raiders from the late 8th c., not the name of every ON speaker. The course stresses traders (Gotland wealth, buried hoards), eastern river routes, and later written sagas (rural aristocrats, a century after the raids) versus earlier poetry. Great warriors as poets is a saga motif. ^[inferred]

**“Old Norse”** is a cover term for Old Icelandic, Old Norwegian, Old Swedish, Old Danish, Old Gotlandic; often used as a synonym of **Old Icelandic** because that is where most texts survive.

**Family-tree caveats (keep):** East/West/North is too clean. ON+Gothic share *jj/ww* “sharpening” (Gothic *ddj/ggw*, ON *ggj/ggw*) and 2sg past *-t*. ON+West Germanic share abstract *-dōm* / *-skapi*, umlaut, and loss of productive reduplication; Gothic *fl-* → *þl-* is East-only. Close contact after splits, not isolation. See [[concepts/germanic-language-family]].

**Homeland:** PGmc often placed in Denmark/southern Sweden; “Battle-Axe Culture” in the 3rd millennium BCE is their archaeological hook — contested elsewhere. ^[ambiguous] Later ON movement: Norway, Orkneys/Shetland, Normandy, Iceland, Greenland, North America, northern England, Rus’ rivers, Byzantium.

**Documents:** runes from the 2nd c. AD (often more archaic than literary ON), mostly Denmark/Sweden. Iceland: Eddic verse (gods/heroes, mainly one MS); **skaldic** praise poetry (rigid metres, kennings; Snorri’s *Prose Edda* as handbook); Ari’s *Íslendingabók*; sagas (kings, settlers, feud/revenge) as entertaining prose with a historical skeleton.

**Lessons 1–2 (now in the wiki):** [[references/islendingabok]] / [[entities/ari-thorgilsson]]; [[references/prose-edda]] / [[entities/snorri-sturluson]]; expansion and longhouse year: [[concepts/viking-age-expansion]]; pronunciation and paradigms: [[concepts/old-norse-grammar]]. Remaining lessons (*Grettis*, *Njáls*, *Egils*, Bjarki, Stamford Bridge, Angantýr, *Vǫluspá*, *Hávamál*) are not yet distilled.
