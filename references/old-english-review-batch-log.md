# Old English review — batches and coverage

[[synthesis/Research: Old English Scholarship 2016–2026|Master review]] · [[references/old-english-2016-2026-literature-map|Cumulative literature map]] · [[skills/incremental-old-english-literature-review|Update procedure]]

**Status:** in progress. B001 remains a pilot. B002 adds a venue-led layer. B003 adds late returns that were not already charted. The register now contains 140 main articles/chapters, 13 books/editions, 2 adjacent experiments, and 6 supplementary items. These groups must stay separate when reporting source counts.

## B001 — October 28, 2026

Broad discovery established 13 overlapping topic families and 10 method families. Main synthesis distinctions: topic versus method; medieval education versus modern teaching research; material manuscript evidence versus edited corpus data; and adjacent experiments versus direct language/literary scholarship. B002 later added names and inscriptions. B003 did not add a family.

**Limit:** a selective public-web starting set. Search-wide retrieval and exclusion counts were not preserved; no retrospective totals can be supplied. Existing records preserve known publication-date differences and evidence scopes.

## B002 — October 28, 2026

Venue-led retrieval added **59 main records**, **1 book** (Majewski on the Ruthwell Cross), and **2 supplementary items** (Martín Arista’s computational encyclopedia entry; Wade’s essay on Bede’s Latin Song commentary). Cumulative main count: **108**. Cumulative records of all groups: **126**.

What changed in the synthesis: names and inscriptions became topic families; the identity family now includes race and global imagination; law, homilies, and glosses qualify the pilot’s poetry-and-corpus tilt. Methods for O’Dell, Fulk, and Leake stay unspecified. Latin-only, Old Norse, reprint, dissertation, and duplicate items were logged and not counted. Homily-collection chapters and several place-name articles remain unopened.

## B003 — October 28, 2026

Late returns from the same retrieval pass, checked against the filed corpus, added **32 main records**, **1 edited book** (Rudolf and Irvine, *The Anonymous Old English Homily*), and **2 supplementary guides** (ROEP pages on the Enchiridion and on anonymous homilies). Cumulative main count: **140**. Cumulative records of all groups: **161**.

Corrections inside existing records: Findell and Kopár are *Fragments* 6, not the volume 7 URL first filed; the encyclopedia entry now carries the DOI printed on the MDPI page; Wade 2020 is *postmedieval* 11(4), 425–433. Upchurch on Leofric was missed in the *Anglo-Saxon England* 48 chart and is now M56. Versloot’s phonology article, previously set aside for lack of an abstract, is L29.

Left out: Brill and Brepols homily chapters still known only by title; JEPNS contents-list items (Wager 2017, Cole 2016, James 2019); Pelle 2024, because the opened paragraph is about the Latin *Visio Pauli*; Fontaine on slave resistance; the isotopic feorm companion; Hough 2016, read and judged not to analyse Old English name elements. Already-charted records were not removed when a second pass would have excluded them.

## B004 — planned

Open the Brill 2020 and Brepols homily-chapter abstracts, and the JEPNS articles that are still contents lists. Notes and Queries and *Review of English Studies* items whose abstracts were not retrieved remain open. This is a retrieval plan, not a claim that these areas lack scholarship. No recurring schedule has been created.
