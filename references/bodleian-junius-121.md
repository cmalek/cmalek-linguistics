# Bodleian Library MS Junius 121

Medieval Manuscripts in Oxford Libraries, “MS. Junius 121” (Summary Catalogue 5232). [medieval.bodleian.ox.ac.uk](https://medieval.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/catalog/manuscript_6320). The same description is summarized by EM1060 at Stanford.

## Key claims

- The book is a Worcester bishop’s collection: canon law, penitentials, and homilies from the commonplace-book tradition of Archbishop [[entities/wulfstan-of-york]] (d. 1023).
- It was produced at Worcester a generation after that archbishop’s death, during the episcopate of St Wulfstan of Worcester (1062–1095), and compiled about 1060–1072, alongside Hatton 113 and Hatton 114 (Franzen 1998). Origin is given as s. xi 2; Watson’s range is 1064–1083. The Tremulous Hand of Worcester glossed it in the early thirteenth century.
- Fols. 9r–69v are a numbered series I–XLIII. Arts. 1–4, 6–10, and 13–14 are Jost’s revised *Polity* (II Polity). Other items in the same numbering, including *Episcopus* and the bishop’s daily office, are not *Polity* in Jost’s sense. The series then runs into the Old English penitential.
- The *Polity* block opens at fol. 9r: rubric *BE HEOFONLICUM CYNINGE*, incipit *An is ece cyning wealdend and wyrhta ealra gesceafta*.
- The latest document, the Council of Winchester of 1076, is on a sheet (fol. 4) that may have been added. A trope on fol. 1 is late eleventh century.
- The scribe of the main Old English has been identified, tentatively, as Wulfgeat, *scriptor* of Worcester, the hand also of Hatton 113–114.

## Not the Genesis manuscript

The Junius manuscript that holds the Old English *Genesis*, *Exodus* and *Daniel* is Bodleian MS Junius 11, not this book. Both came to the Bodleian from Franciscus Junius's bequest; the Genesis headnote dates its place in the Bodleian to 1677. See [[references/genesis-a-and-b]]. ^[inferred]

## Limits

This is a catalogue, not an edition. It uses Jost’s chapter numbers. It does not assign the siglum “B” to the book.
