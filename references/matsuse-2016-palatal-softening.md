# Matsuse 2016

Only the abstract page was readable; the PDF was not fetched.

- Front vowels before or after the velar and i-umlaut are the key triggers.
- OE *giefan* was palatalized; Present-Day English *give* is Norse (*gefa*), adopted into the standard. Sound change cannot explain the replacement.
- *tush* (palatalized) versus *tusk* (not): standardization is a social outcome.
