# The Danish Conquest of England

URL: https://www.worldhistory.org/article/2544/the-danish-conquest-of-england/

## Key claims

- Swein returned 1003–1005 and 1006–1007; tribute in 1002, 1007, 1012; Thorkell the Tall in England 1009–1012, then switched to Æthelred. [extracted]
- 1013: northern magnates submit, ASC calls Swein "full king"; Æthelred goes to Normandy; Cnut with Swein, married Ælfgifu of Northampton. [extracted]
- Swein dies Feb 1014; fleet proclaims Cnut; English nobles invite Æthelred back. [extracted]
- 1015–16: Edmund rebels, Eadric Streona joins Cnut; Æthelred dies 1016; Assandun 18 Oct 1016; Edmund makes terms and dies in 1016. [extracted]
- Cnut sole king 1017, marries Emma, executes Uhtred, Eadric Streona, and Eadwig; earls Thorkell, Erik, Godwin. [extracted]
- Does not mention the massacre or Alney. [extracted]

## Limitations

Popular reference; no massacre-to-invasion argument. See [[concepts/danish-conquest-of-england]].
