# Old English Human Fetus Text

Hasenfratz & Jambeck, *Reading Old English*, revised ed. (2011), p. 352 — an excerpt from an Anglo-Saxon medical compilation. Malek facing translation.

## Problem & Motivation

Not heroic verse: scientific/medical OE. Hasenfratz points to Hollis, “Scientific and Medical Writings,” in Pulsiano & Treharne (2001).

## Method / Architecture

Month scheme (brain wrapped in membrane by week six; 365 veins in month two; month three *man butan sawle*; limbs firm in four; *cwica* in five with maternal *witleas* bed-rest; skin/bone six; digits seven; chest/heart/blood eight; ninth: whether she can give birth; tenth: if not delivered, *feorhadl*, often *Tiwes-niht*).

**Notes:** comparatives are always weak — *scytran ond lengran* after the number. Ethical dative *hire*. *Tiwes-niht* = night before Tuesday (day begins at sundown).
