# Later depictions of rulers, writers, and literary scenes

This group contains painted or printed figures, stained glass, a photographic portrait, a heraldic-looking striped image, and literary scenes. Several images show women with swords and severed heads; the image headed “JUDITH UND HOLOFERNES” makes the Judith theme explicit for that item. Other identifications are visual associations. ^[inferred]

A figure labelled “ST. BEDE – THE VENERABLE (672–735 A.D.)” records a later representation and its caption. It does not resolve the date variation in Bede scholarship. Images that resemble Beowulf scenes, rulers, or saints should be compared with their originating artwork records before assigning artists, dates, or named subjects. ^[ambiguous]

These images document reception and illustration more directly than the appearance of the historical people they may portray. ^[inferred]
