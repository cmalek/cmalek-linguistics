# Immaculate Conception, definition and English feast

Wikipedia, “Immaculate Conception.” [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Conception).

## Key claims

- The doctrine says that Mary was free of original sin from the moment of her conception. It is one of four Marian dogmas. It was debated through the Middle Ages and defined on 8 December 1854 by Pius IX in *Ineffabilis Deus*.
- A feast of Mary’s conception began in the Eastern church in the seventh century, reached England in the eleventh, and spread from there. The word “immaculate” was added to the feast’s name only in 1854. Trent had said, without defining the conception doctrine, that Mary was free of personal sin.
- In 1895 Ecumenical Patriarch Anthimos, answering Leo XIII, called the Immaculate Conception and papal infallibility “Roman novelties.”

## Limits

The page does not say that Wulfstan observed the conception feast. “Eleventh century” covers both his lifetime and the decades after his death, when Junius 121 was copied. Presence of a feast is not the 1854 dogma.
