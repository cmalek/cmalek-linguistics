# Updating Old English Dative–Genitives (Vázquez-González 2024)

Juan G. Vázquez-González, “Updating Old English Dative–Genitives: A Diachronic Construction Grammar Account,” *Languages* 9, no. 6 (2024), article 213. [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2226-471X/9/6/213).

The frame is a verb with a dative recipient and a genitive theme. Mitchell’s list of verbal rections has 48 types. Visser’s list is the other starting inventory. Searching the Dictionary of Old English Web Corpus, and checking quotations rather than inferring case from the governor, he reaches 88 verb types and 443 tokens: 19 types and 260 tokens beyond Visser. Forty-four of the types occur once. Four types added from Mitchell, who prints no examples in the list, are *geleanian*, *getruwian*, *dwellan*, and *gedwellan*.

This is one frame, not a government entry for every verb and preposition. It does not read ambiguous case off [[entities/ycoe|YCOE]]. [[entities/paveda|PaVeDa]] remains the 80-verb ValPaL sample for the wider set of frames. The two resources answer different questions.
