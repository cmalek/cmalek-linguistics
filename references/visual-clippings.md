# Visual Clippings (Wikipedia maps and later art)

A dump of pasted PNGs (late Aug–early Sep 2026), mostly Wikimedia maps and later images that sat next to Wikipedia clippings already distilled. Vision sampled across dates; most remaining files are the same kinds of maps. They are **illustrations**, not excavation reports. Three files copied to `attachments/` for reuse.

## Clusters

**East Anglia geography.** Monasteries vs other sites: Elmham, Dereham, Cnobheresburg, Ely, Soham, Exning, Beodericsworth (Bury), Blythburgh, Dunwich, Iken, Rendlesham, Gipeswic (Ipswich), Sutton Hoo; Devil’s Dyke; Fens vs land above 100 m.

![[east-anglia-settlements.png]]

**North Sea homelands.** Red overlay on Jutland/south Scandinavia; labelled Jutland map *Jutes (Eudoses)* / *Over Jerstal (Varines)* / *Angles*; arrows from Jutland toward Frisia and Britain (two orange paths). Use with [[entities/germanic-peoples]], not as a proven migration flowchart. ^[inferred]

**Southern Britain peoples.** Colour map: West Saxons (*Gewisse*) around *Wintuncæastre*; *Cantware* / *Cantwarebyrig*; Isle of Wight *Wihtwara* and *Ytene sæte* as Jute; striped mixed Jute/Saxon toward *Hrofæscæstre* / London; Romano-Britons west (*Aquae Sulis*, *Durnovaria*).

![[jute-saxon-south-britain.png]]

**Phonetics.** Front/back vowel trapezium with *i y ɪ ʏ e ɛ æ a* vs *u ʊ o ɑ ɒ* and a low central *ä*. Teaching IPA, not an OE reconstruction.

![[ipa-vowel-trapezium.jpg]]

**Later art (do not treat as eighth-century evidence).** Stained glass *Alfredus Rex* / *leges Angliae*; reconstructed woman in wool with amber beads; early-modern woodcut “barbarian” king; inscribed bone plaques (hunter, ship); church wall-painting with bow and oversized figure; Whitby Cædmon memorial cross (“fell asleep hard by 680”).
