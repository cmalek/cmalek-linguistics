# Kijak 2022

Full text read (PDF). Section 2 is a compact survey of the data, mostly after Minkova.

- Triggers: a front vowel and/or *j* in the same syllable; blocked next to a back vowel or consonant (Hogg 1992a: 253).
- Examples: *dæges*, *drȳge* ([ɣ] → [j]); *gieldan*, *giellan*, *georn* ([ʝ]); *gyldan*, *gylden*, *gēs* (velar, before umlauted vowels).
- Minkova 2003: [ɣ] split into /j/ and /ɡ/ in the mid tenth century; evidence from alliteration of *gēar* and *gold*. Later Scandinavian loans complicate /ɡ/ (*gear* < ON *gervi*).
- Kijak's own claim (Element Theory): velars before umlauted vowels were never palatalized, because the velar and the etymologically back rounded vowel share the |U| element. Affrication and vowel unrounding both come from a Middle English ban on |I|+|U| in one segment.

**Limits:** theory-internal; phonetic intermediate stages are not argued for.
