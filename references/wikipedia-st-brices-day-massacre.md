# St Brice's Day massacre (Wikipedia)

URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Brice%27s_Day_massacre

## Key claims

- Sources: ASC, Frideswide charter, William of Jumièges (men, women, children; later exaggeration), Henry of Huntingdon (only men in some towns), William of Malmesbury (only source for Gunhild and husband Pallig). [extracted]
- Williams and Roach: a general extermination is implausible; the Danelaw stayed calm; Æthelred later granted land near Oxford to a Dane, Toti. [extracted]
- Oxford, St John's College: 37 bodies, nearly all male, aged 16–25; radiocarbon 960–1020. Ridgeway Hill: 54 beheaded men, link speculative. [extracted]
- Readings: Stenton (political provocation of Swein's 1003 invasion); Keynes (sympathetic; mercenaries turned on employers); Howard (answer to mercenary treachery); Yorke ("hard-hitting reply"); Lavelle (frontier towns, ethnic hatred and millenarianism); Roach and MacDonald (suspicion and division contributed to Cnut's accession).
