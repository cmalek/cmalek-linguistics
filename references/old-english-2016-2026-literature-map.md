**Batches B001–B003, cumulative review in progress.** See [[references/old-english-review-batch-log]] for additions and coverage.

**Window:** January 1, 2016–October 28, 2026. **Main corpus:** 140 articles and chapters. Books/editions (13), adjacent experiments (2), and supplementary items (6) are separate. IDs are stable within this map. Topic and method labels are reviewer assignments supported by the source surface described; they are not quality rankings.

**Abstracts are an agreed basis for classification** when they identify both topic and methodology. The reading-scope column records exactly what was used. P10 and M13 use an author interview/summary and remain especially provisional. No complete-paper reading or human-read attestation is implied.
