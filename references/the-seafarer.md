# The Seafarer

> [!tldr] First-person OE poem: the speaker can tell a *soðgied* of winter sea-watch, then turns from land-joys to *Meotod* and the withering of earthly *blæd*.

## Problem & Motivation

Classroom text in Mitchell & Robinson (2012). Malek’s facing version (June 2026) is a working translation with grammatical notes, not a published edition.

## Method / Architecture

Opening: *Mæg ic be me sylfum soðgied wrecan* — ice-bound feet, night-watch at the prow, hail as *corna caldast*, swan/gannet/curlew instead of hall-laughter. The land-dweller *ne wat* this. Cuckoo as *sumeres weard* that also *sorge beodeð*. Later: three fates (sickness, age, sword) before the last day; best memorial is praise of the living; *Meotudes egsa*; *Dol bið se þe him his Dryhten ne ondrædeþ*.

**Notes worth keeping:** *yþa* ~ ModE *eddy* via Verner-like ð→d is a student guess. ^[ambiguous] Accusative + infinitive *hlimman sæ*. Partitive genitive *ne ænig hleomæga*. Work paused in the facing file at *Forþon* / *brucað þurh bisgo* (mid-June 2026).
