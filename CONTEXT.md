# Old English Research Mapping

Vocabulary for mapping scholarship on Old English across language, literature, manuscripts, reception, and teaching.

## Language

**Old English research**:
Scholarship directly concerned with Old English language, texts, their material transmission, or their later study, reception, translation, and teaching. Historical and archaeological work belongs here when its connection to Old English evidence is substantive.
_Avoid_: Treating all early medieval English history as Old English research.

**Topic**:
The subject or problem a publication studies, such as word order, manuscript production, religious poetry, or modern reception. A publication can have several topics.
_Avoid_: Using a critical theory or research procedure as its only topic.

**Methodology**:
The approach and procedures used to investigate the subject, such as comparative philology, corpus analysis, palaeography, or a classroom case study. A critical framework and a concrete analytical procedure can both be recorded, but should be distinguished.
_Avoid_: Treating all humanities research as one undifferentiated qualitative method.

**Main corpus**:
The retained journal articles and scholarly book chapters in the agreed publication window. Books, digital resources, proceedings papers, and adjacent experimental studies are recorded separately.
_Avoid_: Counting every linked resource as an included paper.

**Read scope**:
The portion of a source actually inspected for classification: metadata, abstract, identified sections, or complete text. Access to a full text does not establish that the whole work was read.
_Avoid_: Equating bibliographic verification with full-text appraisal.
