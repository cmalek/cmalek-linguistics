# Old Norse Numerals, Negatives, Strong Verbs I-II and Adverbs

Extends [[concepts/old-norse-grammar]]; examples in [[references/egils-saga-eirik-court-passage]]. Ablaut background: [[concepts/germanic-sound-change]].

## Distributives and negatives
*hvárr* "which of two", *hverr* "each", *hvárrtveggja* "each of two", *báðir* "both" (plural only, gen. *beggja*). Negatives: *nein-* and the common *engi* (neut. *ekki*, gen. *einskis/engis*; *eigi* "not", *aldri* "never").

## Numerals
1-4 decline as adjectives (*tveir/tvær/tvau*, *þrír/þrjár/þrjú*, *fjórir/fjórar/fjǫgur*); 5-20 are indeclinable; decades are *tigir* (*þrír tigir* 30); *hundrað* = 120 in the "long" count (*tíu tigir* 100), *þúsund* = 1200, though often used loosely like English. Ordinals mostly weak adjectives (*þriði, fjórði, tuttugandi*).

## Strong verbs
- **Class I** *í - ei - i, e* (*bíta, beit, bitu, bitinn*); stem-final g may drop (*stíga: steig/sté*).
- **Class II** *jú/jó - au - u/o* (*skjóta, skaut, skutu, skotinn*); present singular shows *ý*; *fljúga: fló/flaug*.
- 2sg past ending -st assimilates with t-stems: *beizt, skauzt*. Middle voice (-sk/-mk) forms in the paradigms: *bízk, skýzk*.

## Adverbs
From neuter accusative (*hátt* "loudly"), suffix -a (*illa*, *harðla*), local suffixes *-i* rest, *-an* from, *-gat/-nig* toward (*hingat*). Suppletive comparison: *vel, betr, bezt*; *mjǫk, meir, mest*; *illa, verr, verst*. Poetic enclitic negatives (*-a, -at, -gi*): *vas-k-a* "I was not".
