# Old Norse Nouns, Adjectives, Articles and Participles

Extends [[concepts/old-norse-grammar]] (lessons 1-2) with lessons 3-4. Illustrated in [[references/grettis-saga-glam-fight]] and [[references/njals-saga-gunnars-last-stand]].

## Nouns
- **r-stems**: *faðir, móðir, bróðir, dóttir, systir*; plural i-umlaut, singular u- or i-umlaut obliques.
- **nd-stems**: frozen present participles (*bóndi, frændi*).
- Other consonant stems: *maðr, fótr, vetr, nátt, tǫnn, kýr*, plural in -r, with umlaut.
- **Weak (n-) stems**: *an/jan* (masc. -i, neut. -a: *bogi, hjarta*), *ōn/jōn* (fem. -a: *saga, kona* gen. pl. *kvenna*), *in*-stems (abstract fem.: *elli*). Strength is lexical for nouns.

## Demonstratives and article
*sá* ("that"/"the"; plural = "they"), *sjá* "this", *hinn*. Definite article *inn* is mostly **postposed** (*skálanum*) or free before an adjective (*inn blindi maðr*); double marking is common (*sá hinn blindi maðr*).

## Adjectives
Strong = indefinite, weak = definite; situational, not lexical. Weak without article in epithets (*Hákon góði*, *Helga in fagra*). Predicates are strong. Comparison by -ar-/-ast- or umlaut -r/-st (*langr, lengri, lengstr*), plus suppletives. Possessives derive from pronoun genitives (*minn, þinn, sinn*, reflexive for any number; 3rd person uses genitive *hans, hennar, þeira*).

## Relatives, interrogatives, indefinites
Relative particle **er** (earlier *es*), also *sem*; *þar er* "where", *þá er* "when". *hverr* "who/which of many" vs *hvárr* "which of two"; indefinites *allr, sumr, nǫkkurr, einhverr*.

## Participles
Present in -ndi (declines like comparatives; adverbial use *gekk þegjandi*). Past: strong -inn, weak -ð; passive for transitives, active for intransitives (*kominn*). **Supine** = neuter sg. participle with *hafa* (*hafði vegit*). Prepositions: dative for rest/source, accusative for motion.
