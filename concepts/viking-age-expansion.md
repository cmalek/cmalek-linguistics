# Viking-Age Expansion

EIEOL lessons 1–2 (Krause & Slocum). “Viking Age” in Western records ~790; they put earlier Swedish/Gotland movement ~700 into the east Baltic.

## Key Ideas

**Sequence (their classroom timeline):** Gotland/Sweden traders (and some all-male warrior graves) in Latvia/Estonia ~700 → Norwegian Lindisfarne 793 and raids in Britain/Ireland → Orkney/Shetland colonies ~800 (farming, fishing, seals) → Dublin 836 (ports, not inland empire); Isle of Man as Norse–Celtic mix → Iceland 860–870, ~60 years of settlement, ~20,000 people; *Egils saga* land-claim by beacons → Danes in NE England ~870, later [[concepts/danelaw]] (open: army occupation vs follow-on settlers) → Normandy 880–920 (Rollo called Norwegian; place-names look Danish) → 980s combined raids; 994 London bought off at **16,000** pounds silver; 1002 St Brice’s Day; Svein; 36,000 pounds; 1013 conquest; Cnut → Eiríkr the Red Greenland 982 (~3,000); Bjarni 986; Leifr Vinland 1003.

**Farm, not only raid (lesson 2):** raiding is seasonal. Dairy + grain (more grain in the south); hunting/fishing/seals/walrus/whale for food and trade (hides, ivory, oil). Turf walls with wooden roofs where timber is scarce; elsewhere vertical or horizontal timber. Longhouse 40–100 ft, ship-hull curve, two post-rows, raised side benches, central hearths; animals later in separate buildings. Jarlshof (Shetland): second longhouse, cobbled stable, smithy with slag. Year: melt → plant barley/oats, peat/wood; May–June lambs, shear, upland *seter*; July–Sep hay and harvest, slaughter surplus; winter indoor work, cows stalled.

Fyrkat (Denmark) ~980 ring-fort house reconstruction is illustration, not Iceland.
