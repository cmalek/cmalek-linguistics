# Danish Conquest of England (1013–1017)

## Sequence

1. 1003–07: [[entities/swein-forkbeard]] raids; tribute 1002, 1007, 1012. Thorkell the Tall 1009–12.
2. 1013: northern submission, ASC calls Swein "full king"; Æthelred flees to Normandy. Cnut is with Swein.
3. Feb 1014: Swein dies; fleet proclaims [[entities/cnut-the-great]]; English nobles recall Æthelred, who drives Cnut out of Lindsey.
4. 1015–16: [[entities/edmund-ironside]] rebels, then leads after Æthelred dies (23 Apr 1016). Eadric Streona goes over to Cnut.
5. 18 Oct 1016: Assandun, Cnut wins; Edmund makes terms and dies 30 Nov 1016 (search summary of Wikipedia; Alney partition not in [[references/worldhistory-danish-conquest-of-england]]).
6. 1017: Cnut sole king, marries Emma, executes Uhtred, Eadric Streona, Eadwig; earls Thorkell, Erik, Godwin.

## Link to 1002

The massacre is not in the chronology of [[references/worldhistory-danish-conquest-of-england]]. Its causal role is an interpretation: see [[concepts/st-brices-day-massacre]].

## Wulfstan

Wulfstan wrote Æthelred's 1014 law code (VIII Æthelred), preached the *Sermo Lupi* in 1014, then stayed on to draft Cnut's laws. See [[entities/wulfstan-of-york]], [[concepts/sermo-lupi-ad-anglos]].
