# Sermo Lupi ad Anglos

Dated 1014 by the Nero A.i rubric; possibly first preached 16 February 1014. Scholars range 1010–1016. It explains raids as God's punishment, with the Britons in Gildas as the model, and appears in five manuscripts that Wulfstan kept revising. A phrase recurs in II Cnut, so one hand drafts homily and law across [[entities/aethelred-the-unready]]'s and [[entities/cnut-the-great]]'s reigns.

The ROEP summary read here does not mention the 1002 massacre. Whether the sermon alludes to it is open; see [[synthesis/Research: St Brice's Day Massacre]].
