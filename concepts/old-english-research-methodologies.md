# Methodologies in [[concepts/old-english|Old English]] research

[[synthesis/Research: Old English Scholarship 2016–2026|Review overview]] · [[references/old-english-2016-2026-literature-map|Full literature map]] · [[concepts/old-english-research-topics|Topic guide]]

Methodology answers how a question is investigated. These ten overlapping families retain the specificity of humanities methods. “Qualitative” alone is too coarse to distinguish an examination of scribal hands, a queer reading of reception, and interviews with teachers. Batch 2 did not add a method family. Batch 3 did not either. Both enlarged philology, historical contextualization, close reading, and material analysis, because many new records are documentary, epigraphic, or source-comparative. Three homily and computus articles stay outside every method family: their publisher pages did not state a procedure.

## Framework, procedure, and evidence

A framework such as material feminism or Cultural Linguistics guides interpretation. A procedure such as close reading, manual coding, a manuscript comparison, or interviewing describes the analytical work. A corpus, manuscript, poem, or classroom is an evidence source. Record these separately where the abstract permits; a named framework does not automatically establish particular procedures.

Computational work is not one method either: annotation design (L06), recurrent phrase retrieval (L11), authorship profiling (S01), and lexicon simulation (S02) have different purposes and evidential limits. Combining counts and interpretation does not automatically make a paper a formally designed mixed-methods study.

## Abstract coding rule

Use an explicit method statement where available. If the abstract describes only a subject or conclusion, mark the method unspecified until another authorized source clarifies it. Broad family labels in this map are reviewer classifications; exact procedural claims require corresponding source evidence. Methodological quality and weaknesses were not assessed from abstracts.
