# Emotional interaction and persuasive design

Chapter 6 examines emotion across a user's experience, rather than treating it as a decorative layer on top of usability. Sounds, colors, icons, and virtual agents can communicate state or evoke feelings. Interfaces can also cause frustration when apparently simple actions become difficult.

## Distinctions

| Approach | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Expressive interface | Convey a state or evoke a response |
| Affective computing | Recognize or express emotion through computing |
| Emotional AI | Infer feelings or behavior from sensed information |
| Persuasive design | Encourage a change in behavior |
| Anthropomorphism | Attribute human qualities to a nonhuman object |

The chapter discusses sensing expressions, voices, and physiological signals as approaches to inferring affect. Such an inference should not be recorded as direct access to a person's feelings. ^[inferred]

[[concepts/ux-data-at-scale]] raises questions about collecting and interpreting personal traces. [[skills/ux-evaluation-planning]] provides a way to investigate whether an expressive or persuasive design produces the intended experience.
