# Kurdi Lexical Semantics

From Kurdi (2017), *NLP and Computational Linguistics 2*, ch. 1. Lexical semantics sits between semantics and lexicology: meaning of words and how it shifts.

## Key Ideas

- Context can be linguistic (*cotext*) or register (formal / vulgar). Words also stand in paradigmatic relations (synonym, antonym) and may be bundles of semantic features.
- **Denotation:** stable, community-shared conceptual content (philosophy: the set of referents). **Connotation:** secondary, often affective meanings. *Red* denotes a colour and connotes heat, blood, love, stop, expulsion, communism depending on collocation. Register splits (*dog / mutt / pooch*) share denotation, not connotation.
- Everyday figurative uses (*the minister fought*, *racked her brain*, *crazy* of a driver) are extensions, not “wrong” uses.
